Rule coverage

What the checker actually tests

Rules are deliberately focused on data that can be validated from the BOM itself. Heuristics that would pretend to know electrical suitability or real-time sourcing status are excluded.

Reference designators

Expands common numeric ranges, detects duplicates within a row and across rows, highlights suspicious RefDes formatting, and identifies placed/DNP conflicts.

Quantity consistency

Checks for blank, non-numeric, fractional, zero, and negative quantities, then compares placed quantity with the number of unique reference designators.

Part identity

Checks manufacturer, MPN and internal-part-number data, flags placeholders, and detects conflicting manufacturer, value, footprint, or internal-P/N mappings.

Assembly fields

Reviews footprint/package, description/value coverage, interprets common DNP/DNI terms, and keeps DNP items visible without treating them as normal placement rows.

Sourcing consistency

Checks supplier-P/N mappings and flags lifecycle values already present in the BOM such as EOL, obsolete, NRND, or last-time-buy for manual sourcing review.

Duplicates and cleanup

Detects exact duplicate lines and identifies same-part rows that may be candidates for consolidation without automatically changing the BOM.

Practical workflow

How to use the report

01

Start with errors

Resolve duplicate reference designators, quantity mismatches, missing required identity fields, and invalid quantities before reviewing softer warnings.

02

Confirm column mapping

An incorrect mapping can create false results. This is especially important when ERP exports use internal names such as “Item”, “Part No.” or “Locations”.

03

Review DNP logic

Make sure DNP/DNI flags are explicit and unambiguous. A positive design BOM quantity can coexist with DNP, but the assembler needs a clear placement decision.

04

Check sourcing identity

For procurement, the combination of manufacturer and MPN should identify the exact component. Do not rely only on a generic description such as “10 kΩ resistor”.

05

Export the reviewed BOM

The reviewed CSV preserves original data and adds checker status, issue summaries, normalized designators, detected reference count, and DNP status.

06

Still perform engineering review

A BOM checker cannot verify stock, lifecycle, authenticity, electrical ratings, footprint geometry, polarity, AVL policy, or manufacturability. Those require engineering and supply-chain review.

What this tool intentionally does not claim to verify

Real-time lifecycle or stock status, counterfeit risk, electrical suitability, voltage/current/temperature ratings, package dimensions, symbol-to-footprint correctness, PCB connectivity, polarity/orientation, feeder compatibility, stencil design, DFM/DFA, approved-vendor policy, compliance, or final production readiness.
